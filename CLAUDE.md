# Kafa Medical Supplies LTD — Website

Public marketing and product website for a Kenyan medical supplies company.

**This is not** an e-commerce checkout, a dashboard, or an admin portal. There
are no carts, no prices on the site, and no user accounts. Every product
enquiry routes to the contact form as a quote request.

## The company supplies exactly four products

1. Medical Gloves
2. Injection Supplies
3. IV Drips and Infusion Supplies
4. URIT-82 Blood Glucose Meter

Nothing else. If a change would imply the company supplies something outside
this list, stop and ask.
