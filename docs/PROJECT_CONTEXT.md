## Business model

Quote-based, not transactional. A customer browses products, then sends an
enquiry describing what they need and in what quantity. The team replies with a
quote.

This is why the site has no prices, no cart, and no checkout. Product pages end
in a "Request a quote" call to action that deep-links into the contact form
with a prefilled subject.

## Customers

Hospitals, clinics and pharmacies in Kenya.

## What this site is not

- Not a dashboard or admin portal
- Not a patient-facing medical service
- Not a source of medical advice
