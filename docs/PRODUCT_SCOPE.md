# Product Scope

The authoritative list of what Kafa Medical Supplies LTD sells. This file is
the single source of truth for product scope — do not restate this list in
other docs.

## The four products

The company supplies exactly these four products.

### 1. Medical Gloves

> Examination gloves for use in hospitals, clinics and healthcare facilities.
> Contact us to confirm sizes and quantities.

### 2. Injection Supplies

> Syringes, needles and related injection supplies. Contact us to confirm the
> specifications and quantities you need.

### 3. IV Drips and Infusion Supplies

> IV drips, infusion sets and related consumables for patient care. Contact us
> to confirm availability and quantities.

### 4. URIT-82 Blood Glucose Meter

> The URIT-82 blood glucose meter for blood glucose testing. Contact us for
> current availability and pricing.

Descriptions are deliberately conservative: no specifications, certifications,
medical claims, or manufacturer relationships. Naming the URIT-82 states which
product is stocked; it claims no affiliation with the manufacturer.

## No product categories

The site previously had a category layer (`/products/[category]`) with seven
categories, all empty. That structure is removed. The Products page lists the
four products directly.

Do not reintroduce categories, category navigation, category types, or a
dynamic product route without an explicit decision from the owner.

## Explicitly not supplied

Never mention these as things the company supplies. They were removed from the
site on purpose.

- Laboratory Equipment
- PPE / protective wear
- Diagnostic Devices as a product line
- Blood pressure monitors
- Any product not in the list of four above

Also never imply:

- Operation beyond Kenya (East Africa, "multiple countries", regional reach)
- Supplier certifications, accreditations or verified-supplier status
- Partnerships, distributorships or manufacturer affiliations
- Customer counts, order volumes, years of experience, or any statistic

## Writing product copy

Allowed: what the product is, who uses it, and an invitation to make contact.

Not allowed: sizes, materials, gauges, volumes, accuracy figures, sterility
claims, regulatory approvals, clinical benefit claims, or brand names other
than URIT-82.

When you need a fact you do not have, ask. Do not fill the gap.
